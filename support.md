# Support

Email [{{ site.contact_email }}](mailto:{{ site.contact_email }}). We answer within 2 business days.

It helps to include:

- what you expected and what the page showed,
- the error message, if any,
- whether the export came from PostgreSQL, MySQL, SQL Server or Oracle.

Please **don't send your export file or report**: they contain names and email addresses. If a specific row is the problem, describe it or replace the names first.

See also the [documentation](./), [privacy policy](privacy.html) and [security policy](security.html).
